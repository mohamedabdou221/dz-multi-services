## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, premium, and intensely reassuring visual sanctuary engineered for the Comorian and West African diaspora, alongside their local communities and families. The core promise—"Plus qu'un service, une solution"—demands an aesthetic of quiet authority, unshakeable stability, and deep human warmth. 

The visual style blends **Contemporary Editorial Warmth** with **Modern Institutional Clarity**:
- **Tone & Mood:** Trustworthy, stately, dignified, and familial. The product acts as an elevated bridge across borders, eliminating transactional friction and uncertainty.
- **Visual Tenets:** Generous breathing room, authentic warmth through a natural cream canvas (never sterile clinical white), crisp structural layouts, rich deep bordeaux accents, and restrained tactile depth.
- **Contextual Integrity:** In strict accordance with brand guidelines, transactional flows focus on care and facilitation rather than retail commerce. Direct public pricing is omitted; the interface prioritizes bespoke quotation requests, status tracking, and the heartfelt gesture of "Commander pour un proche" (Ordering for family back home).

## Colors

The palette balances formal stature with organic comfort, purposefully avoiding cold modern tech grays in favor of warm, tactile earth tones.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Burgundy (`#8C0028`):** The primary anchor. Applied to dominant calls-to-action, key headlines, accent markers, and active state indicators.
- **Deep Black (`#101010`):** The foundation for high-contrast primary typography, persistent navigation, luxury dark-mode accents, and grounding footer structures.
- **Warm Cream (`#F5F3EC`):** The default root canvas tone. Imbues the platform with physical substance reminiscent of heavy cotton paper.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved exclusively as an elevated layer for content containers, cards, and input fields atop the cream background.
- **Charcoal Muted (`#2B2B2B`):** Secondary text, descriptive metadata, and subtle inline icons.
- **Inverse Crimson (`#BD023B`):** Vivid dynamic accent used solely for brand mark accents, badge highlights, and interactive states against deep black surfaces.
- **Structural Border Tint (`#E2DDD0`):** The definitive soft separator for cards and containers sitting on the cream background.
- **Status Green (`#2F6B3F`):** Deep emerald reserved for validated milestones, confirmed dispatches, and successful handovers.
- **Alert Amber (`#8A5200`):** Rich ochre amber communicating pending estimations, quotes awaiting review, or document verifications.

## Typography

The typographical pairing reinforces confidence and functional legibility across varied devices. 

- **Display & Headings (Montserrat):** Geometric, resolute, and modern. Weights 700 and 800 instill institutional credibility in key slogans, value propositions, and section titles. Weight 600 delivers clear visual hierarchy within nested cards and modals.
- **Body & Controls (Work Sans):** Highly legible, neutral, and balanced. Work Sans supplies structural support with open letterforms, preventing eye strain when entering sensitive identity, customs, or delivery details.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large display headings (`display-hero`) use negative letter-spacing for tight editorial presentation. Body text must maintain relaxed leading (`1.5` to `1.6`) to provide effortless reading under varying ambient lighting conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid system bounded by a maximum content container width of 1280px, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px), page margins `margin: 3rem` (48px) to `space-xl` expansions.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px), canvas margin `2rem` (32px).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem` (16px), canvas margins `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` (20px).

### Spacing Rhythm
Spacing relies on an 8pt base grid for structural integrity:
- Micro spacing (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px) controls internal component density (icon-to-label gaps, button padding).
- Structural spacing (`space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px) orchestrates card interior paddings, stacked form rows, and cross-section layout intervals.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic, grounded appearance, this design system avoids exaggerated synthetic drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **Tonal Tiering** accompanied by fine **Structural Outlines**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Surface (Level 0):** The primary canvas in Warm Crème (`#F5F3EC`). Ground level for browsing and storytelling.
2. **Elevated Cards & Sheets (Level 1):** Solid Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) containers framed with a crisp, low-contrast 1px border (`#E2DDD0`). Shadows are barely perceptible: `0 2px 8px rgba(16, 16, 16, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus (Level 2):** Modals, flyouts, and dropdowns rendered in pure white, bounded by `#E2DDD0`, supported by a soft grounding shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(16, 16, 16, 0.08)`.
4. **Subdued Dark Containers:** Reserved for persistent bottom panels, header navigational highlights, and diaspora reassurance portals. Formed with Deep Black (`#101010`) surfaces with micro-borders of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system communicates both professionalism and friendliness through measured, tailored corner curves.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** Standardized at `12px` (equivalent to `0.75rem`), yielding an ergonomic, friendly handfeel during field input and tactile pressing.
- **Containers & Content Cards:** Scaled between `16px` and `20px` (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`). The larger outer radii embrace child elements naturally and soften analytical dense layouts.
- **Pill Accents:** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, micro-badges ("Devis en attente", "Confirmé"), and auxiliary filter tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#8C0028`, label in `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600, border-radius `12px`. Heights: 48px desktop, 44px mobile. Focus states reveal an external 3px ring of `rgba(140, 0, 40, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary (Diaspora / Proche):** Deep Black (`#101010`) background with `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F3EC` typography. Used specifically for secondary hero choices such as "Commander pour un proche".
- **Outline / Tertiary:** Transparent background, 1.5px border of `#8C0028` with `#8C0028` text, transitioning on hover to an ultra-soft `#8C0028` 6% opacity fill.

### Cards & Form Containers
- Constructed on `#FFFFFF` atop the `#F5F3EC` canvas.
- Perimeter border of `1px solid #E2DDD0` with `16px` to `20px` radius.
- Padding: 24px desktop, 16px mobile.
- Section divisions inside cards use an understated horizontal divider of `1px solid #F5F3EC`.

### Input Fields
- White background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E2DDD0` border, `12px` border-radius.
- Internal padding: `12px 16px`. Label set in `#2B2B2B` (14px, weight 600).
- Focused state: Border changes to `#8C0028` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(140, 0, 40, 0.12)`).
- Prefix / Phone Selectors: Dedicated Comorian (`+269`) and regional dial codes cleanly segmented by a vertical 1px divider in `#E2DDD0`.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Success ("Confirmé", "Livré"):** Background tint `rgba(47, 107, 63, 0.10)`, text `#2F6B3F`, font size 12px, font weight 600, pill-shaped.
- **Pending Quote ("Devis en attente"):** Background tint `rgba(138, 82, 0, 0.10)`, text `#8A5200`, font size 12px, font weight 600, pill-shaped.
- **Service Categories:** Clean white chips with `#E2DDD0` border and `#2B2B2B` text; active state flips to `#8C0028` solid background with white text.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radio / Checkbox boxes: 20px x 20px, 1.5px border in `#2B2B2B`.
- Checked state: `#8C0028` fill with crisp white vector marks. Border transitions to `#8C0028`.
- Touch target: Centered within a minimum 44px x 44px invisible tap surface for mobile compliance.

### Narrative Reassurance Banner ("Commander pour un proche")
- Elevated banner component pairing deep black (`#101010`) or deep bordeaux (`#8C0028`) with `#F5F3EC` typography. 
- Integrated icon badges utilize the Inverse Crimson (`#BD023B`) for localized highlights, accompanied by clear timeline tracking checkpoints rather than monetary cart counters.
